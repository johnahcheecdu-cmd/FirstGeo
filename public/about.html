About Us

Advanced Geospatial Technology.
Experienced People. A Different Perspective.

First Geospatial is a majority Indigenous-owned Australian geospatial technology company based in Darwin and Katherine, Northern Territory.

Who We Are

A majority Indigenous-owned geospatial technology company

We bring together expertise in drone operations, LiDAR scanning, aerial mapping, surveying, civil engineering, ICT, project management and spatial data management to deliver practical solutions for government, industry, researchers and communities.

Our focus is simple: capture accurate spatial information, turn it into meaningful insights and help our clients make better decisions.

From infrastructure and environmental monitoring to cultural heritage mapping and immersive digital experiences, we combine advanced technology with experienced professionals who understand the complexities of working across remote and regional Australia.

Our Story

Different backgrounds, shared ambitions

First Geospatial came together through a series of unexpected connections.

Some of us have known each other since school. Others have worked together professionally, crossed paths through industry or connected through projects completely unrelated to geospatial technology.

What started as conversations between friends and colleagues quickly developed into something more.

We discovered that, collectively, we had an unusually complementary range of experience: Indigenous technology leadership, drone aviation, professional surveying, civil engineering, ICT systems, major infrastructure delivery, business development and complex operational management.

More importantly, we shared an interest in technology, innovation and finding better ways to solve real-world problems.

The opportunity became clear. By bringing our individual expertise together, we could establish a geospatial business capable of supporting clients across multiple industries while developing new technologies with genuine community value.

First Geospatial was built around that shared opportunity: combining the right people, the right expertise and a common ambition to do something meaningful.

What We Do

Professional drone and geospatial services across Northern Australia

We provide professional drone and geospatial services supporting projects across the Northern Territory and broader Australia. Our capabilities bring together advanced aerial data acquisition, specialist technical expertise and experienced project delivery.

  • Drone Surveying & Aerial Mapping High-resolution aerial imagery, photogrammetry, site mapping, 3D modelling and spatial data capture.
  • LiDAR Scanning & Terrain Mapping Aerial LiDAR data acquisition supporting terrain modelling, vegetation analysis, environmental monitoring and infrastructure planning.
  • Civil Engineering & Infrastructure Geospatial support for engineering, construction, transport infrastructure, asset management and project planning.
  • Environmental & Land Management Aerial surveys, landscape monitoring, vegetation mapping, change detection and research data collection.
  • Cultural Heritage & Sacred Site Mapping Culturally appropriate spatial imaging and documentation, undertaken with the involvement and authority of relevant Traditional Owners.
  • GIS & Geospatial Data Services Spatial datasets, mapping, data management and technical solutions supporting informed decision-making.
  • Drone Inspections & Monitoring Aerial imaging and data collection supporting infrastructure inspections, construction progress monitoring and asset assessment.

Our approach combines technical capability with practical project management, allowing us to support everything from targeted data acquisition through to complex multidisciplinary projects.

Virtual Country

Connecting technology, culture and Country

One of the opportunities that helped bring our team together was the development of Virtual Country, an immersive geospatial technology platform being developed by First Geospatial.

Virtual Country explores how modern imaging, mapping and digital technologies can help people document, preserve, manage and experience significant places and landscapes.

By combining high-resolution aerial imagery, 360-degree photography, immersive video, spatial mapping and other geospatial datasets, Virtual Country aims to create interactive digital representations of Country.

These experiences have potential applications in cultural heritage preservation, education, environmental research, community engagement and maintaining connections to Country for people who may not be able to physically visit important locations.

Indigenous Data Sovereignty

Communities retain authority over their own information

An important part of Virtual Country is recognising that capturing information does not automatically give the organisation collecting it the right to own, distribute or control it.

This is particularly important when documenting sacred sites, culturally significant landscapes, traditional knowledge and community heritage.

We are developing Virtual Country around principles of Indigenous data sovereignty, with the aim of providing secure digital environments where authorised communities and Traditional Owners can determine how their cultural information is stored, accessed, shared and experienced.

This includes exploring culturally governed digital keeping places, controlled access to sensitive imagery and opportunities for communities to retain authority over digital representations of their Country.

Our ambition is not simply to capture Country in digital form, but to help create the tools through which communities can preserve and share that information on their own terms.

Virtual Country represents an important part of First Geospatial’s long-term vision: bringing together commercial geospatial expertise, technology development and meaningful community applications.
