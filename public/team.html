The People Behind First Geospatial

Our People

Leadership and technical expertise

First Geospatial was built around a shared opportunity, bringing together people with different backgrounds and an unusually complementary range of experience.

Managing Director & Indigenous Technical Lead

Johnathan Ah Chee

Johnathan Ah Chee brings extensive experience in information and communications technology (ICT), technical development, environmental management and engagement with Indigenous communities, government and research organisations. His background combines technical expertise with established relationships across the Northern Territory, including Traditional Owners, environmental organisations, Parks and academic networks.

As Managing Director and majority Indigenous owner of First Geospatial, Johnathan leads the company’s strategic direction, technical development, Indigenous governance and community engagement. He plays a central role in ensuring the business delivers culturally appropriate geospatial services while supporting Indigenous employment, technical capability development and data sovereignty.

Johnathan is also the lead developer of Virtual Country, First Geospatial’s immersive digital platform, having developed its initial prototype. His technical leadership supports the company’s ambition to combine advanced geospatial technology with culturally governed digital experiences that strengthen connections to Country.

Director & Operations and Program Lead

Mark McLean

Mark McLean brings more than 10 years’ experience in operations, programme delivery, project coordination and stakeholder management across government and private-sector environments. He has extensive experience delivering complex programmes in remote Northern Territory settings, coordinating dispersed teams, operational systems, logistics, risk and large-scale workforce activities.

At First Geospatial, Mark leads operational planning, project and programme management, tender coordination, costing and scheduling, business systems, resource planning and project mobilisation. His role is focused on turning commercial opportunities into well-planned, deliverable projects and building the operational systems required for First Geospatial to scale sustainably.

Mark is also a Director of First Geospatial and contributes to the company’s governance, commercial strategy and development of its geospatial and remote-field capability.

Founder & Commercial Lead

John Avenell

John Avenell brings experience in business development, commercial strategy, stakeholder engagement and relationship management, with a focus on identifying opportunities, building strategic partnerships and supporting business growth. His established commercial networks, including connections across the Northern Territory business community, contribute to First Geospatial’s market development and commercial positioning.

At First Geospatial, John leads business development, client engagement, commercial partnerships and opportunity generation. He plays a key role in identifying government and private-sector opportunities, developing tender and procurement pathways, and establishing relationships with industry partners and specialist service providers.

John also contributes to the company’s long-term commercial strategy, including the development of scalable geospatial services, specialist processing capabilities and new market opportunities. His focus is on building a sustainable commercial pipeline and positioning First Geospatial as a competitive Indigenous-owned business operating across the Northern Territory and broader Australian markets.

Chief Pilot, Surveying & Geospatial Specialist

Bill Mitchell

Bill brings over a decade of experience in professional surveying, drone operations and geospatial data acquisition, with extensive expertise across Northern Australia and remote operating environments.

His background spans major infrastructure developments, mining and resources projects, civil construction, Defence infrastructure and large-scale government works. He has held senior technical and operational positions with national surveying consultancies and major civil construction companies, delivering complex surveying and UAV projects across diverse environments.

Bill’s specialist expertise includes aerial mapping, high-accuracy spatial data acquisition, GPS and GNSS surveying, machine control, construction surveying and UAV-based volumetric analysis. He also brings extensive experience in establishing and managing commercial drone operations, including CASA regulatory approvals, aviation safety systems, operational procedures and technical training.

Combining advanced technical knowledge with practical field experience, Bill brings a strong understanding of the accuracy, safety and operational requirements involved in delivering professional geospatial services across Australia’s most challenging environments.

Project Manager & Civil Engineer

Rod Jeffrey

Rod Jeffrey brings more than 30 years of experience in civil engineering, project management and executive leadership, delivering major infrastructure, transport, Defence and construction projects across Australia and internationally. A Chartered Professional Engineer with a Bachelor of Engineering (Civil), Bachelor of Business and MBA, Rod has held senior leadership positions with major engineering and construction organisations, including Sitzler, BMD Constructions and John Holland.

His extensive experience includes directing complex, multimillion-dollar infrastructure projects, managing multidisciplinary teams, overseeing engineering design and technical assurance, and delivering projects in challenging regional and remote environments. Notably, Rod served as Project Director for Darwin’s Tiger Brennan Drive Overpass Project and Melbourne’s Webb Dock International Container Terminal development.

At First Geospatial, Rod provides senior project management and civil engineering expertise, supporting infrastructure and geospatial projects through technical oversight, project planning, engineering coordination and delivery assurance. His experience in surveying and drone capability development, major project delivery and commercial management strengthens First Geospatial’s ability to deliver integrated solutions for government, Defence, infrastructure and private-sector clients.
