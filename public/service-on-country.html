Remote & On-Country Drone Services

Remote & On-Country Drone Services

Indigenous-owned drone services built to deploy to remote communities and on-Country locations across the Northern Territory, working alongside the people who hold knowledge of that Country.

Who This Is For

For communities, Traditional Owners and organisations working on remote Country

This service is for Aboriginal communities, Traditional Owners, Land Councils, Ranger groups and organisations who need drone and geospatial support delivered to remote locations, with cultural authority and community direction guiding how the work is carried out.

What You Receive

Deliverables & outputs

  • Remote-deployed aerial capture. Drone imagery and data capture coordinated for remote and on-Country locations.
  • Country mapping & monitoring. Mapping outputs that support cultural, land and sea management documentation and monitoring.
  • Community-directed delivery. Project scope and access arranged with, and directed by, the relevant Traditional Owners and community representatives.

Our Workflow

How we deliver the project

  • Scope with community input. Project goals, access and cultural considerations agreed with community representatives before planning begins.
  • Plan remote logistics. Travel, equipment and timing arranged for the location.
  • Capture. Aerial data captured on-Country, on community terms.
  • Deliver. Data and outputs handed over in line with the access and data arrangements agreed with the community.

Where We Operate

Darwin, Katherine & the Northern Territory

We’re based in Darwin and Katherine, with projects delivered across the Northern Territory and remote deployment arranged for on-Country and regional sites. For projects outside the NT, we scope national delivery on request.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who directs how the work is carried out?

Cultural authority and the direction of the relevant Traditional Owners and community representatives guide how we scope and deliver on-Country work.

How do you handle sensitive site data?

We’re developing a dedicated Indigenous Data Sovereignty product for the secure, permission-based handling of imagery and data about Country. See our Community & Data Sovereignty section for details. In the meantime, data handling for each project is agreed directly with community partners.

Can you deploy to remote communities?

Yes. Remote deployment across the Northern Territory is part of how we work. Let us know your location and timing when you enquire so we can plan logistics.
