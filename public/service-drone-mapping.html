Drone Mapping & Photogrammetry

Drone Mapping, Aerial Imagery & Photogrammetry

High-resolution aerial imagery processed into orthomosaics, point clouds and terrain models for mapping, planning and monitoring projects across the NT.

Who This Is For

For projects that need an accurate, current picture of a site

This service suits planning, construction, agriculture and land-management projects that need an up-to-date, accurate picture of a site, from a single orthomosaic for planning purposes to repeat capture for monitoring change over time.

What You Receive

Deliverables & outputs

  • Orthomosaic imagery Stitched, geo-referenced imagery of the full site.
  • 3D point clouds & terrain models Photogrammetry-derived point clouds and digital terrain or surface models.
  • Volume & measurement data Stockpile volumes, distances and area measurements where the project calls for them.
  • Progress & change monitoring Repeat capture to track site progress or change over time.

Our Workflow

How we deliver the project

  • Scope & plan We confirm the site, ground control needs and deliverables before flight planning.
  • Capture Overlapping aerial imagery captured to suit the processing method and required resolution.
  • Process Photogrammetric processing into orthomosaics, point clouds and terrain models.
  • Review & deliver Outputs checked against the brief and delivered in your required format.

Where We Operate

Darwin, Katherine & the Northern Territory

We’re based in Darwin and Katherine, with projects delivered across the Northern Territory and remote deployment arranged for on-Country and regional sites. For projects outside the NT, we scope national delivery on request.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What’s the difference between photogrammetry and LiDAR?

Photogrammetry builds 3D data from overlapping photos and works well for open, well-lit sites. LiDAR can see through vegetation gaps to the ground and performs better under dense cover. We’ll recommend the right method for your site during scoping.

Can you measure stockpile volumes?

Yes, where the site and brief call for it. Let us know during your enquiry so we can confirm feasibility and accuracy requirements.

How often can you re-fly a site for monitoring?

That depends on your project’s needs, from a single capture through to a regular monitoring schedule. We’ll work out a cadence that fits your timeline and budget.
