Drone Infrastructure & Asset Inspections

Aerial inspection imagery for infrastructure, remote sites and hard-to-access assets across the Northern Territory, without the cost and risk of manual access.

Who This Is For

For asset owners who need a safer way to inspect hard-to-reach infrastructure

This service suits infrastructure owners, utilities, local government and industry who need to inspect towers, structures, remote assets or large sites without the cost, downtime or risk of manual access such as rope access or scaffolding.

What You Receive

Deliverables & outputs

  • High-resolution inspection imagery. Close-range imagery of structures and assets for visual condition assessment.
  • Site & asset orthomosaics. A geo-referenced overview of the asset or site for records and reporting.
  • Organised inspection imagery. Imagery organised against the asset or area it covers, ready for your engineers or maintenance team to review.

Our Workflow

How we deliver the project

  • Scope & plan. We confirm the assets, access, airspace and any CASA approvals required.
  • Capture. Close-range inspection imagery captured around the structure or site.
  • Organise. Imagery indexed and organised by asset or location.
  • Review & deliver. Imagery and any supporting overview handed over for your team to assess.

Where We Operate

Darwin, Katherine & the Northern Territory

We’re based in Darwin and Katherine, with projects delivered across the Northern Territory and remote deployment arranged for on-Country and regional sites. For projects outside the NT, we scope national delivery on request.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you inspect towers and tall structures?

Yes, subject to site access, airspace and CASA approvals. We assess these during scoping before confirming the flight.

Is drone inspection safer than manual access?

Drone inspection removes the need for workers to physically access height or confined areas for an initial visual assessment, which is often safer and faster, though some findings may still need a follow-up physical inspection.

Do you provide thermal inspection?

Thermal inspection can be arranged where the project calls for it. Let us know your requirements when you enquire so we can confirm we can deliver it for your site.
