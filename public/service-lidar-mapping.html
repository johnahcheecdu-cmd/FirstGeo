Drone LiDAR Mapping

Drone LiDAR Mapping: Darwin & Northern Territory

Aerial LiDAR capture for terrain, vegetation and infrastructure projects, flown from our Darwin and Katherine bases across the Northern Territory.

Who This Is For

Built for teams who need accurate elevation and terrain data

Drone LiDAR mapping suits government agencies, engineering and survey teams, land managers and researchers who need accurate terrain, vegetation structure or built-asset data. Particularly across terrain that’s difficult or costly to survey from the ground, including remote and vegetated sites across the Northern Territory.

What You Receive

Deliverables & outputs

  • Classified point clouds Ground, vegetation and structure classification suited to your project’s required specification.
  • Digital terrain & surface models DTMs and DSMs derived from the captured point cloud.
  • Vegetation structure data Canopy height and vegetation density outputs for environmental and land management projects.
  • Project-ready file formats Data delivered in the formats your GIS, CAD or engineering software requires, with a capture report outlining the quality checks performed.

Our Workflow

How we deliver the project

  • Scope & plan We confirm site access, required accuracy and deliverables before planning the flight.
  • Capture Aerial LiDAR capture flown under CASA-compliant drone operations.
  • Process & classify Point cloud processing, classification and quality checks against the agreed specification.
  • Review & deliver Data and reporting handed over in your required format, with a chance to flag anything before sign-off.

Where We Operate

Darwin, Katherine & the Northern Territory

We’re based in Darwin and Katherine, with projects delivered across the Northern Territory and remote deployment arranged for on-Country and regional sites. For projects outside the NT, we scope national delivery on request.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What accuracy can drone LiDAR achieve?

Achievable accuracy depends on the sensor, flight height and site conditions. We discuss the accuracy your project needs during scoping and confirm it’s realistic before capture.

Can you survey vegetated or remote sites?

Yes, LiDAR is particularly useful where dense vegetation or difficult access limits traditional ground survey. We arrange remote deployment across the NT as part of the project plan.

Do you provide a surveyor’s sign-off?

Where a project requires registered survey certification, we can coordinate with a licensed surveyor as part of project delivery. Let us know your requirements when you enquire.
